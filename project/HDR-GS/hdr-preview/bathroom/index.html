Extended Reinhard tone mapping and an sRGB display transform make the HDR rendering viewable on ordinary screens. The MP4 is an SDR visualization; the downloadable EXR preserves the original float32 HDR radiance.

One fixed calibration is used for the entire video. The camera continues spiraling in the same direction through the forward/backward transition and loops smoothly.
